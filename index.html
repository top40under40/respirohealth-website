The Problem

Respiratory disease in India
is caught too late.

Tuberculosis, COPD, asthma, and bronchitis are among India's most significant public health burdens. Most patients present at an advanced stage of disease - not because early signs weren't there, but because the tools to act on them early don't reach where patients are. Acoustic biomarkers in a cough carry real diagnostic signal, but no model has ever been validated under the real-world conditions - OPD noise, consumer microphones, WhatsApp's audio compression - that actual deployment requires.

How It Works

One voice note.
An acoustic risk score.

Respiro's screening model works entirely through a cough recording sent over WhatsApp - no app, no device, no clinical infrastructure. Every step is designed to work in the exact conditions it will be deployed in, not a controlled lab.

Step 1
Cough Capture

Patient sends a 15–20 second voluntary cough recording to a WhatsApp number - no app download, any smartphone

Captured in the same acoustic domain as deployment - WhatsApp's Opus compression, real ambient noise

Same protocol whether captured directly by a patient or during structured intake at a partner OPD

Step 2
Acoustic Risk Scoring

The model analyses acoustic features of the cough and generates a respiratory-disease risk score

The disease-specific model is being trained exclusively on confirmed-diagnosis recordings - GeneXpert for TB, spirometry for COPD/asthma - never provisional impressions

A pre-screening signal only, never a diagnostic claim - every clinical decision stays with a treating physician

Step 3
Confirmatory Routing

High-risk individuals are flagged for confirmatory molecular or clinical testing before they would otherwise self-present

Enables earlier detection at population scale, with no new clinical infrastructure required

Repeat recordings across a patient's treatment journey support longitudinal monitoring research

Reaches every Indian with a respiratory concern through a single WhatsApp message - no app, no clinic visit, no new infrastructure.

Why WhatsApp

The infrastructure already exists.
In every patient's pocket.

Every health-tech company builds a patient app. Patients don't download it. Respiro's acoustic screening model is built on the channel patients already use - making adoption a non-issue from day one.

Every previous acoustic respiratory AI has been trained on controlled laboratory recordings and validated on the same. Deployed in real-world clinical settings — with background OPD noise, consumer-grade microphones, and WhatsApp's Opus audio compression — accuracy collapses. Respiro trains exclusively on WhatsApp-captured audio from real hospital OPDs, linked to molecularly confirmed diagnoses. The model learns from the same acoustic conditions it will be deployed in.

The Vision

Starting with the disease burden
India carries the most.

Respiro starts with tuberculosis - India's largest respiratory disease burden - and builds toward a screening model that covers the full spectrum of respiratory disease, at national scale.

01

India carries 25% of the world's tuberculosis burden. Our first disease-specific model targets TB screening, trained on molecularly confirmed diagnoses.

02

The same acoustic model and dataset extend to COPD, asthma, and other respiratory conditions - multi-disease classification from the same cough recording.

03

Multi-site data collection across India's diverse geographies and demographics - built for generalisability from day one, not tuned to one hospital's patient population.
